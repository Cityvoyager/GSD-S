## What we deliver

- Results-based M&E frameworks aligned with GBF, Paris Agreement, SDG 6, and donor performance requirements
- Indicator design, data collection tools, and reporting systems for GIZ, EU, KfW, and multilateral programmes
- Programme evaluations using documentary review, stakeholder interviews, and technical & financial data analysis
- Learning systems that capture lessons and feed into global knowledge platforms and policy dialogue
- Adaptive management approaches for complex, multi-country programme environments

## Project evidence

- **GIZ BioClime – Team Leader (2024–present):** Designed M&E methodological framework for the biodiversity–climate–water nexus across Thailand, Indonesia, and Uganda
- **GIZ SecBlue – Team Leader (2025–present):** Established monitoring and learning approach for NbS on water security; results-based reporting aligned with GCF
- **GIZ COMIFAC (2021–2022):** Evaluated the "One Health" programme component (€5.6M) in Central Africa
- **EU DG INTPA – Cameroon (2019–2021):** Annual implementation and completion reports for EU-funded environmental projects; aid effectiveness assessments
- **Dorsch Impact (2023–present):** Designed internal M&E framework and knowledge management system for the Climate, Environment & Biodiversity portfolio
