## What we deliver

- Case studies, policy briefs, PANORAMA-type solutions, and learning formats for international dissemination
- Capacity building for partner organisations to systematise and share learning experiences
- EU policy monitoring, analysis, and stakeholder engagement strategies for legislative processes
- Aid coordination frameworks and dashboards supporting multi-donor programme coherence
- Webinars, podcasts, infographics, and practitioner guidance for virtual outreach

## Project evidence

- **GIZ BioClime (2024–present):** Produced and disseminated PANORAMA-type learning products; delivered capacity-building for partner organisations
- **GIZ SecBlue (2025–present):** Developed "Guidelines for Scaling up" handbook, policy brief, and infographics; designed webinar and learning series
- **EU DG INTPA – Cameroon (2019–2021):** Built EU aid coordination dashboard; authored policy report on clandestine gold mining with economic impact analysis
- **Austrian Presidency of the Council of the EU (2018–2019):** Chaired Working Party of Environment sessions; led negotiation of Council Conclusions
- **European Commission DG ENV (2017):** Contributed to EU Circular Economy Action Plan; produced case studies and best practices
