## What we deliver

- Initial and ongoing ESG due diligence for development finance institutions and private sector clients
- Compliance verification against EIB Standards, EU taxonomy, and IFC Performance Standards
- Independent accountability and complaints mechanism support — analysis of alleged ESG violations
- Data-driven compliance reports synthesising ESIAs, monitoring data, and stakeholder inputs
- Gender, displacement, and access-to-information compliance assessments

## Project evidence

- **EIB – Kenya:** Compliance review of gender and displacement impacts of the Akiira geothermal power plant
- **EIB – Bulgaria:** ESG compliance review of a municipal waste incineration plant (€67M loan)
- **EIB – India:** Social compliance review of the Pune Metro Rail project (€600M loan) — evictions and gender aspects
- **EIB – Lebanon & Slovakia:** Governance and environmental compliance reviews across renewable energy and nuclear sectors
- **EU DG INTPA – Cameroon:** Gender compliance review of an EU-funded project following a sexual abuse allegation
