## What we deliver

- Team leadership and expert coordination for GIZ, EU, KfW, World Bank, and UNDP-funded programmes
- Technical and financial offer preparation for publicly tendered consulting services
- Work planning, expert day allocation, quality assurance, and risk management
- Backstopping and contract management for climate, environment, biodiversity, and water projects
- Liaison with commissioning authorities, implementing partners, and EU Delegations
