## What we deliver

- AI-assisted reporting, data structuring, and synthesis for complex, multi-source programme datasets
- Dashboard design and indicator tracking systems for real-time programme monitoring
- Database management and data analytics for accountability and transparency reporting
- Decision-support tools and automated workflows for project management and M&E processes
- AI workflow integration for consulting teams across multilingual, multi-donor environments

## Project evidence

- **EU DG INTPA – Cameroon (2019–2021):** Built data dashboards aggregating financial and technical data from all EU-funded projects
- **EIB Accountability Mechanism (2021–2022):** Structured large volumes of ESIA, monitoring, and contractual data into data-driven compliance reports
- **Dorsch Impact (2023–present):** Designed knowledge management system and data analytics tools to monitor acquisition efforts
- **GSD-S (2022–present):** AI-assisted service delivery, performance monitoring, and engagement with Regen Network on blockchain-based impact verification
- **GIZ BioClime & SecBlue (2024–present):** AI prompting applied to M&E data aggregation, synthesis of NbS learning, and multilingual reporting
