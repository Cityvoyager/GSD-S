Our Services

What we deliver

  • ESIAs aligned with EU Directive, EIB Environmental and Social Standards, and IFC Performance Standards
  • Project screening and scoping across energy, water & sanitation, transport, and natural resource management sectors
  • Compliance reviews assessing adherence to national legislation and international agreements throughout the project lifecycle
  • Stakeholder consultation frameworks and public participation processes
  • Environmental and social management plans (ESMPs) and monitoring protocols

Project evidence

  • EIB – Slovakia: Compliance review of EIA for a nuclear power plant modification (€60M loan) — assessed public participation and stakeholder consultation processes
  • EIB – Germany: Compliance review of EIA for the Autobahn A49 highway project (€264M loan) — assessed procedural and substantive EIA compliance
  • EIB – Lebanon: ESIA compliance review for two wind energy projects (89 MW & 67 MW, €120M loan)
  • EU DG INTPA – Cameroon: Oversight of ESIAs for national parks and natural resource management projects under EDF and EU Trust Fund for Africa
  • EIB: Certified in EIB Environmental and Social Principles and Standards for EIB-Financed Projects (2021–2022)
